# Personal Website

Johan Sardar's personal site: who he is, what he has built, and what he is into.

## Language

**Portrait**:
The single photo of Johan at the top of the home page, cropped tight around his face.
_Avoid_: Avatar, headshot, hero image

**About Statement**:
One short first-person statement about Johan on the home page, such as where he studies or where he is from.
_Avoid_: Bio, bullet, tagline

**Project**:
One thing Johan has built, which lives on its own separate site that this site points to.
_Avoid_: Work, case study, portfolio item

**Placeholder Project**:
A Project with no real content yet, present only so layout and motion can be judged.
_Avoid_: Dummy, mock, stub

**Project Pill**:
A wide rounded bar that stands for one Project, used on both the home page and the Projects page.
_Avoid_: Card, rectangle, tile

**Interest Statement**:
One short first-person statement on the Interests page about something Johan cares about outside his work.
_Avoid_: Hobby, topic, collection
